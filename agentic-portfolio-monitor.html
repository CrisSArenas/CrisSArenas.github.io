Look-through: single companies

Stocks and bonds in the same company count together. That is why a portfolio can sit neatly inside every asset-class range and still hold too much of one name.

Step 5 of 6

You decide

Each finding comes with a fix, its cost and the portfolio as it would look after the trade. A fix that breaks another rule never reaches you. Stale prices block automatic trading at every setting, and private assets are never sold to fix a range, so those go to the investment committee.

Step 6 of 6

The audit trail

An institutional portfolio needs proof of every action. Every finding, decision and trade is written to a hash-chained log: each line’s fingerprint is computed from the line and the fingerprint before it, so changing any entry breaks every fingerprint after it. Try tampering with one.

Guide: Agentic Portfolio Monitoring

What the page does

It runs a $1bn multi-asset book through one day of events. Add market events, run the checks, read what they find and approve or reject each fix. The audit trail at the end shows if anyone edits the record later.

How much runs on its own

Observe only finds and explains problems and never trades. Propose, you approve drafts and costs every fix and waits for you. Small trades run automatically releases fixes under $25m that aren't critical and pass the post-trade check.

Stale prices block automatic trading at every setting. Private assets are never sold to fix a range, and those findings go to the investment committee.

After each approval

The book changes, so it gets checked again. Fixes that are no longer needed are withdrawn and the rest are resized.

What is simulated

The portfolio and the events are made up. No model is called and no order is placed.

How it works

What is checked, and the limits
  • The book has eight asset classes, including private equity and real assets carried at quarterly marks.
  • Each cycle checks policy ranges, drift, tracking error, two-week liquidity, capital call coverage, issuer concentration across stocks and bonds, and price freshness.
  • Each finding comes with a proposed fix, its trades, its cost and the tracking error after the trade.
  • Small fixes can run on their own if you allow it. Stale prices block automatic trading at every setting, and private assets are never sold to fix a range.
  • Every action goes into a hash-chained log, so any later edit shows up.

Data and limits

  • The book and the events are made up. Trading costs and liquidity horizons are assumptions.
  • No order is placed and no model is called.
