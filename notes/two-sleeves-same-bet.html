When two sleeves are running the same bet

Low correlation between sleeves can hide a shared factor bet until a crowded unwind. Factor loadings show it earlier.

Cristian Arenas28 October 2026Test it in the Multi-Strategy Builder

Two hedge fund sleeves can show low correlation for years and still carry the same factor bet. The overlap shows up in a crowded unwind, when both fall together.

Look at loadings

Regress each sleeve's returns on a few factors such as equity, momentum, value, credit and rates. Two sleeves overlap when both load heavily on the same factor. In the Multi-Strategy Builder, equity long/short carries 0.32 momentum and quant carries 0.62. The equity mandate says it has no style tilts.

Split calm and stress

Average correlation between the sleeves is 0.10 in calm months and 0.47 in stress months. A covariance matrix built on the full history understates how the book behaves when it matters. The worst stress episode in the demo costs the book 17.0% on today's weights.

Watch for drift

Overlap can grow. The macro sleeve in the demo added a momentum overlay in its last few years, and its calm-month correlation with quant rose from 0.14 to 0.68. Comparing recent loadings with long-run ones catches this before the next unwind.

What to do with it

Treat two overlapping sleeves as one position when you size them. In the demo that means cutting quant, the weakest and most crowded sleeve, and moving the risk to credit.
