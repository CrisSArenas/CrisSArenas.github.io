Enterprise research platform

Alternative-Data Research Brain

Built for institutional investment teams
Single-tenant, deploys in the fund's own cloud
Licensed alternative-data feeds

A production-grade research platform that gives an investment team the same depth of alternative-data coverage on five hundred names that a single analyst could previously sustain on five. It ingests licensed data feeds across the coverage universe, has a language model read and score every item, and produces a cited, position-ready research note in which every claim is bound to a source document or it does not ship. It then surfaces connections across feeds that no single-source review would reveal, and reconciles that read against reported fundamentals and market data.

The system is complete and deployment-ready for a fund. It installs inside the customer's own cloud account, so holdings, watchlists and research output never leave their perimeter. The panels on this page are live and run the platform's real control flow.

The problem it solves

Sell-side research is a commodity. Every fund reads the same notes, hears the same calls, and books the same expert-network hours. The differentiated signal sits outside that loop, in licensed data most teams already pay for and few read systematically: hiring patterns that front-run a reorganisation, consumer panel data that contradicts guidance, product telemetry that shows an enterprise account in trouble, trade press that names a customer months before it is disclosed.

The obstacle is not access, it is throughput. A thorough alternative-data review costs an analyst most of a day per name. That does not scale to a coverage universe, so in practice the data gets consulted at earnings and during diligence, and ignored the rest of the year. The subscriptions are paid for and the signal decays unread.

This platform makes that review continuous and universe-wide, and it does so under a constraint that governs the entire design: an investment professional must be able to audit every sentence back to a source document. A research tool that is occasionally confidently wrong is worse than no tool, because it costs more to check than to replace.

Degradation is a first-class state, not an error page. Feeds are isolated from one another, so a vendor outage, a throttle, or an expired entitlement removes that feed's contribution and nothing else. The run completes on what remains, the note is written over the surviving evidence, and the coverage gap is reported to the analyst rather than hidden. Ingestion never happens inside a user request, so no upstream vendor can slow the terminal down.

Platform architecture

Four tiers, all deployed inside the customer's cloud account. The only egress is to licensed data vendors and to the model provider through a private endpoint, both under the fund's own contracts.

The separation that matters most is between ingestion and reading. Ingestion is a scheduled, idempotent, per-name job that writes an immutable evidence snapshot. Everything a user sees is a read of that snapshot. That is what makes the terminal fast, the output reproducible, and an audit answerable: the exact evidence set behind any note can be reconstructed months later, because it was never assembled on the fly.

The data layer

Eight licensed feed classes, chosen so each sees something the others structurally cannot. The platform is vendor-agnostic: each class is an interchangeable connector behind a common evidence contract, so a fund substitutes its existing subscriptions rather than buying a prescribed stack.

Entitlements are enforced in the platform, not in the vendor contract

Data licences differ in what may be stored, for how long, whether derived content may be redistributed, and which internal groups may see it. Those terms are encoded per connector and enforced at ingestion and at read time. A user without entitlement to a feed does not see its items, does not see notes that depend on them, and the coverage gap is disclosed rather than silently filled. Retention windows are applied per feed, so expiring content leaves the evidence store on schedule and any note resting on it is marked as no longer reproducible.

What the platform deliberately does not ingest. No material non-public information channels, no scraped sources whose terms prohibit it, and no personal data beyond what a licence explicitly permits. Feed classes were selected partly on whether their compliance posture is defensible to a fund's legal function, because a signal a firm cannot legally act on has negative value.

Cited synthesis, and the validator that gates it

The note is organised around the decision a portfolio manager actually makes rather than as a topic-by-topic recap. Every claim sentence must resolve to a real ingested document. Click any citation below to open the underlying item.

Inject a hallucination forces the failure this platform exists to prevent: a confident, well-written claim citing a document that does not exist. The validator checks every citation against the evidence set, rejects the synthesis, and a bounded repair pass runs with the specific error returned to the model.

Evidence-bound synthesis
Click a citation to inspect the source document behind it.

Four independent controls stand between the model and the analyst:

  • Closed-world generation. The model may assert only what the supplied evidence supports, and is required to declare thin coverage rather than infer around it. Snapshot chat answers "the current evidence does not show that" instead of falling back on training knowledge.
  • Citation validation with bounded repair. Demonstrated above. If repair also fails, the platform keeps the prior valid output and raises an operational alert rather than shipping a weaker note.
  • Independent adjudication of connections. Covered in the next section: proposing a link and judging it are separated.
  • Presentation-layer re-verification. The terminal re-checks every citation against the evidence set before rendering it as a link, so a control failure upstream still cannot produce a clickable false reference.
No buy or sell rating, by design. The platform sees evidence, not the fund's model, position sizing, mandate or risk budget. A rating would be wrong by construction and would invite exactly the unearned trust the rest of the design works to prevent. It states what the evidence supports, with citations and an explicit confidence, and leaves the decision with the investment professional.

Cross-feed connection discovery

This is the capability that justifies the platform over reading each feed separately. Statistical similarity is good at finding documents that share vocabulary and bad at distinguishing a genuine link from topical adjacency, so similarity only nominates candidates. A separate adjudication step evaluates each candidate in isolation against an explicit standard, and returns a confidence the analyst can gate on.

Two stages rather than one for three reasons. Economics: exhaustive pairwise evaluation grows quadratically with the evidence set and is unaffordable at universe scale, while nomination keeps it near-linear. Quality: a narrow question about two documents produces a decisive judgment where a broad question about forty produces hedging. Auditability: every surfaced connection carries its own recorded rationale and confidence, so a PM can interrogate why it appeared.

The divergence is the point. Agreement between ground-level evidence and reported numbers is confirmation and rarely tradable. Disagreement, where hiring and panel data move one way while guidance moves the other, is where the platform earns its cost, and it is surfaced as a first-class output rather than left for the analyst to notice.

Deployment, security and governance

Single-tenant, inside the customer perimeter

The platform installs into the fund's own cloud account as a single-tenant deployment. There is no shared infrastructure and no cross-customer data path, because for an investment team the watchlist is itself sensitive: which names are covered, and how attention shifts between them, leaks strategy. Storage is encrypted with customer-managed keys, network paths to vendors and the model provider are private, and secrets are injected at runtime rather than built into images.

Identity and access

Authentication federates to the fund's existing identity provider over SAML or OIDC with multi-factor enforcement, so joiners and leavers are handled by existing processes rather than a separate user list. Authorisation is role-based and evaluated per request, combining the user's role with the entitlement set of every feed contributing to what they are reading. Sessions are short-lived, and an unauthorised request fails cleanly rather than degrading into a partial view.

Audit and model governance

Every model interaction is logged in full, immutably, with the evidence set, the generated output, the validator verdict and any repair attempt. That record is what makes the system governable under a model risk framework: a reviewer can answer what the system said, on what date, on what evidence, which controls fired, and who read it. Because notes are generated from immutable snapshots, any historical output can be reproduced exactly. Prompt and model-version changes are themselves versioned, so a shift in behaviour is attributable to a specific change rather than to drift.

Scale and operating envelope

  • Concurrency. Feeds for a name are ingested in parallel, and names are processed independently with no shared state, so the practical ceiling is vendor rate limits and the model throughput the fund has contracted, not the architecture. Adding capacity is horizontal.
  • Scheduling. The universe is swept on a cadence per feed class, with event-driven refreshes on filings and news spikes so a material development does not wait for the next scheduled pass.
  • Failure isolation. A failed document read degrades to a title-level record; a failed synthesis preserves the prior valid note and alerts; a failed adjudication drops one connection. No single failure escalates into a failed run.
  • Observability. Per-feed health, coverage completeness, control-failure rates, latency and cost per name are exported to the fund's existing monitoring, so the platform is operable by the team that runs everything else.
  • Integration. Notes and connections export to research management and collaboration tooling over an authenticated API, so output lands in the workflow analysts already use rather than adding another destination.

Honest limitations

  • It reads, it does not decide. There is no rating, no sizing, no portfolio context, and that is deliberate. It compresses the time cost of evidence review; the judgment remains entirely with the investment professional.
  • Coverage is bounded by licences. The platform is only as good as the feeds a fund subscribes to. Thin coverage on a name produces a thin note that says so, which is correct behaviour and occasionally disappointing.
  • Connection precision needs continuous measurement. The adjudication gate reduces false links substantially but does not eliminate them. A human-graded evaluation set per sector is the right instrument, and building that out is the main open work.
  • Language models change underneath you. Provider updates shift behaviour. Version pinning and the change-attribution logging above manage this; they do not remove the need to re-baseline periodically.
