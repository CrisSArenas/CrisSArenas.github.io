Data foundation

Data model

GP reports, portals, administrator files and market data land in one normalized model with checks, before any agent or analytic touches them. Every demo reads and writes these entities. Field names follow the cash flow and fee concepts in the ILPA reporting and performance templates where they apply.

Entities and relationships

Click an entity to jump to its fields.

Fields

Amounts are in fund currency, signed from the LP's point of view (contributions negative, distributions positive) unless noted. Dates are ISO 8601. Every value extracted from a document carries a citation.

Mapping to ILPA template concepts

ILPA's updated Reporting Template adds a gross-to-net management fee reconciliation and more granular expense and carry reporting; its Performance Template asks for gross and net IRR and TVPI with and without subscription line effects. Both apply to funds from Q1 2026 or launching from January 2026 (Alston & Bird summary). The mapping below is by concept, not by template line number.
