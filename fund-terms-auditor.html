04 · Fund terms and fees

Fund Terms Auditor

An endowment holds a $45m commitment to a fictional 2019 buyout fund, Ostrel Capital Partners VI. Four quarterly reports in the ILPA format have arrived since the fee step-down. This auditor reads the LPA and the reports, recomputes every fee, offset and carry figure from the terms, and cites both sides of each break: the clause and the report line. Which breaks to raise, and whether to re-up, is the team's call.

The hard problem
Checking that fees, expenses and carry match the LPA, and seeing IRR without subscription line effects.
What the system takes off the team
Reading the LPA and every report, recomputing fees, offsets and accrued carry quarter by quarter, rebuilding net returns without the line, and drafting the query letter.
What stays with the team
Which breaks to raise, and whether to re-up.
Method
Management fee recomputation on the LPA basis, fee offsets with the gross cap, whole-fund (European) and deal-by-deal (American) waterfalls, hypothetical-liquidation carry accrual, XIRR with and without the subscription line, Monte Carlo of remaining exits.
Data
Synthetic LPA excerpt, reports and cash flow ledger for a fictional fund; seeded simulation of future exits. Or your own deal ledger and fee lines, processed locally.
Where AI is used
An LPA terms extractor, a report extractor, a recomputation agent, a breaks agent and a letter drafter, with every break approved by a person before it reaches the letter.
Limitations
One LPA excerpt and four reports; real LPAs run to a hundred pages with side letters. Expenses are not tested against a cap. The pipeline is a simulation; no model is called.

        Methodology, assumptions and limitations

        Method

        • Management fee. Recomputed each quarter from the LPA: the investment-period rate on total commitments, then the post-period rate on net invested capital at the start of the quarter, times the LP's share. Net invested capital is rebuilt from the deal ledger and compared with the report.
        • Offsets. The LPA offset percentage times the fund's transaction, monitoring and directors' fees, times the LP share, capped at the gross fee.
        • Carry. Accrued carry is what the GP would receive if the fund were liquidated at reported NAV (LPA section 9.4), run through the whole-fund waterfall in section 9.1: capital, an 8% preferred return compounding annually, a full GP catch-up, then 80/20. The American toggle runs the same tiers deal by deal with no netting.
        • Subscription line. LP flows are rebuilt as if capital had been called on each investment date. The multiple does not change; the IRR does.
        • Remaining exits. Each unrealized deal exits at a uniform time over the next four years at its mark times a lognormal multiple; future fees accrue on net invested capital until it exits.

        Formulas

        fee (in period) = rate_IP x commitments / 4 x share fee (after) = rate_post x NIC_start / 4 x share offset = min(fee, offset% x portfolio fees x share) pref due += (unreturned + pref due) x ((1 + p)^t - 1) catch-up target = carry x pref paid / (1 - carry) accrued carry = GP total if liquidated at NAV IRR: sum CF_t / (1 + r)^((t - t0)/365) = 0

        Assumptions register

        Limitations

        • Every fund, GP, company and number is fictional. The LPA is a two-page excerpt; real agreements, side letters and MFN elections change the terms an LP is owed.
        • Partnership expenses are read and carried into cash flows but not tested against a cap or an allowed-expense list.
        • The deal-by-deal waterfall does not allocate fees and expenses to deals, which flatters the GP slightly. Clawback and escrow terms are not modelled beyond the end-of-fund comparison.
