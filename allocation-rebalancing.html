How it works

Method, formulas, assumptions and limits
  • Each weight is a sleeve's market value over total assets. A sleeve is in breach when its weight sits outside the policy range.
  • Drift tracking error is the volatility of the gap between current and policy weights under the capital market assumptions below. Private equity and real assets use desmoothed volatility, because appraisal marks understate how much they move.
  • Tactical views are entered in percentage points and sum to zero, with cash taking the remainder. If their tracking error is above the budget, every view is scaled down by the same factor, so the shape of the views is kept.
  • The quarter's spending leaves cash. Capital calls move cash into the private sleeves and distributions move it back. Liquid sleeves outside their range then trade to the nearest edge, or to the tactical target if chosen.
  • Cash is the balancing account. When it falls below its range, the most overweight liquid sleeves are sold toward their tactical targets; when it rises above, the most underweight are bought. Trades below the minimum size are dropped unless they cure a breach.
  • Private sleeves are never traded to rebalance. If private equity stays above its range, an overlay sells US equity index futures for the excess, so the equity exposure counts private equity at its upper limit.

Formulas

breach_i:  w_i < lo_i  or  w_i > hi_i
TE_drift = sqrt(a' Sigma a),  a = w - w_policy
sigma_private = sigma_reported sqrt((1 + rho) / (1 - rho))
k = min(1, budget / sqrt(v' Sigma v)),  tilt = k v,  sum v = 0
change in expected return = tilt' mu
cash_after = cash - spending - sum(calls - distributions) - sum(trades) - costs
costs = sum |trade_i| x cost_i / 10,000,  sum of trades including cash = 0
overlay = (w_PE - hi_PE) x NAV_after

Assumptions

Correlations

Fixed. They come from a four-factor view of equity, rates, credit and real assets, rounded to two decimals.

Data and limits

  • The endowment, its managers and its holdings are made up. Holdings come from the seed, with a drift pattern that leaves private equity overweight after a year of strong private marks.
  • Private marks lag public markets, so the private weights are estimates until the next quarter's statements arrive.
  • One minimum trade size applies to every liquid sleeve. Hedge funds are treated as tradable at quarter end, which assumes the redemption notice has been given.
  • The overlay ignores futures basis, roll and margin. It changes the equity exposure; it does not change the private equity holding itself.
