How it works

Method, formulas, assumptions and limits
  • The LBO runs year by year. Revenue grows, the margin moves in a straight line to its exit level, and free cash flow after interest, tax and capex pays down debt.
  • Exit equity is the exit multiple times final EBITDA, less net debt. The price is fixed at $840m, and each case decides how much of the add-backs to believe.
  • The bridge splits the equity gain into EBITDA growth, AI initiatives, the change in multiple, debt paydown and costs. The pieces add up to the total.
  • AI initiatives enter at the CIM's cost and run-rate uplift, times the odds I give them.
  • The blend compares the fund after a 1.5% fee and 20% carry over an 8% hurdle with a co-invest that pays neither.

Formulas

FCF = EBITDA - capex - nwc x dRev - interest - tax - cost debt_t = debt_t-1 - sweep x FCF equity_H = m_H x EBITDA_H - net debt_H MOIC = equity_H / equity_0; IRR = MOIC^(1/H) - 1 growth = (E_H - u - E_0) m_0; initiatives = u m_0 multiple = (m_H - m_0) E_H; deleveraging = ND_0 - ND_H uplift_t = p x run-rate x ramp(t)

Data and limits
