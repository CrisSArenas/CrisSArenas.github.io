Investment memo 06 · Hedge fund portfolio

Five-sleeve multi-strategy book

Date
June 2026
Portfolio
Five hedge fund sleeves: Quillmere Equity Partners, Tarsen Global Macro, Brennoch Relative Value, Ostvale Credit, Veyl Systematic
Strategy
Multi-strategy book, sleeves weighted by equal risk contribution
Under review
All five sleeve weights; Veyl Systematic (quant) at 14.8% of the book
Prepared by
Cristian Arenas

Recommendation

Cut quant (Veyl Systematic) from 15% to 5% of the book and move the ten points to credit (Ostvale Credit). Hold relative value (Brennoch Relative Value) flat, with no additions, until its duration is back inside the mandate. Turn on volatility targeting and drawdown stops for every sleeve.

Conditions

  • Rerun the book on the new weights before the credit subscription goes in, and check the worst stress episode against today's 17.0%.
  • Brennoch gets a written request to bring its rates loading back under the 0.1 cap in its mandate.

Summary

This reviews our five-sleeve hedge fund book, weighted so each sleeve adds a fifth of the variance, on ten years of monthly returns from July 2016 to June 2026 and the five mandates. The book returned 3.4% a year net, with 4.8% volatility, a Sharpe ratio of 0.31 and a maximum drawdown of 17.0%.

  • Too much of the book sits on one momentum bet. Quant and equity long/short have shared it from the start. Macro has added a momentum overlay its mandate rules out, and macro and quant now correlate 0.69 in calm months, up from 0.13.
  • Quant is the weakest sleeve. It made 3.1% gross and 0.6% net a year on average, with a 36.7% drawdown, and lost most in the worst stress episode.
  • The diversification the book was sized on disappears in stress. Average correlation between sleeves goes from 0.10 in calm months to 0.47 in stress months.
  • Relative value has drifted outside its mandate on duration, and pass-through expenses take most of its gross return.

Track record

Book and sleeves

Over the ten years the book compounded at 3.4% net with 4.8% volatility. Macro led with 6.6% net, a 0.60 Sharpe ratio and a 9.8% drawdown. Relative value made 1.2% and quant 0.1% net, both with negative Sharpe ratios, and together they hold 44% of the capital. Quant's 36.7% drawdown is the deepest in the book.

Calm and stress

Average correlation between sleeves is 0.10 in calm months and 0.47 in the 18 stress months. With the same weights, book volatility is 3.3% on the calm covariance and 8.3% on the stress covariance. The diversification ratio falls from 1.88 to 1.32. In stress, equity long/short and quant correlate 0.67, and relative value and credit 0.76.

The worst stress episode ran 11 months from June 2017. Replayed on today's weights the book loses 17.0%. Quant contributed −5.5%, equity long/short −4.7%, credit −4.6% and relative value −3.9%. Macro made 0.4%.

Factor overlap and style drift

Equity long/short and quant load on momentum at 0.32 and 0.62 over the first seven years. Shared factors explain a correlation of 0.44 between them. Veyl's mandate names momentum; Quillmere's says it has no style tilts. No other pair shares a factor bet.

Between the first seven years and the last 30 months, two sleeves changed style. Macro's momentum loading went from 0.10 to 0.79 (z 5.3). Relative value's rates loading went from 0.08 to 0.78 (z 5.8), against a mandate cap of 0.1. The other 23 sleeve and factor pairs were stable. With macro now on momentum, its calm-month correlation with quant rose from 0.13 to 0.69 (Fisher z 3.0).

Fees

The sleeves made 6.4% gross and 3.5% net a year on average. Total fee drag is 295 bps a year, 46% of the gross return. Pass-through costs 60 bps a year more than standard 1.5% and 20% terms. Relative value goes from 5.2% gross to 1.4% net after a 3.50% pass-through. Netting cost, performance fees paid to winners in years when others lose, is only 6 bps a year.

Portfolio fit

Risk controls

Volatility targeting alone lifts the book to 4.8% a year at the same volatility and cuts the drawdown to 12.2%. Drawdown stops alone give 3.9% and a 12.6% drawdown. With both on, the book returns 4.8% with 4.7% volatility, a Sharpe ratio of 0.60 and a 10.2% drawdown, with 9 stops fired. I want both. Stops act after the loss and sell near the low when a crowded unwind snaps back, and volatility targeting reacts late on a 12-month lookback. The weights use the full ten-year covariance, so the base book is in-sample; the controls do not look ahead.

Exposures and liquidity

The cut shrinks the largest momentum position and the worst stress contributor. Credit is the cleanest sleeve to add to, with calm-month correlations of 0.17 or less with every other sleeve, no drift flag and no shared factor bet. The cost is that credit and relative value correlate 0.76 in stress and will hold more than half the book. Liquidity also slows, from monthly on 30 days notice to quarterly on 90. The book on the new weights has not been measured, which is why the rerun is a condition.

Portable alpha overlay

The overlay tab runs at its own defaults over 180 months, with a generic alpha sleeve. On that path portable alpha compounded at 3.97% against 5.67% for the 60/40 benchmark, with a 34.78% drawdown against 25.44% and an information ratio of −0.45. Across 96 seeds the median active return is +0.3%, and 60% of paths beat the benchmark. I would not add an overlay on this evidence, and this book was not tested as the alpha sleeve.

Diligence findings

The five mandates were read and 26 fields extracted, each tied to its page and line. Against a hand-labeled key, extraction accuracy is 100% with full citation coverage. The review ran 71 checks and raised 4 flags, catching 4 of 4 planted issues with no false flags on 41 clean checks.

  • Global macro's momentum loading rose from 0.10 to 0.79. Line 5 of the Tarsen mandate rules out systematic trend following or a momentum overlay.
  • Relative value's rates loading rose from 0.08 to 0.78. Line 4 of the Brennoch mandate holds rates beta between −0.1 and 0.1.
  • Equity long/short and quant run the same momentum bet. Line 5 of the Quillmere mandate says the sleeve has no systematic style tilts.

The fourth flag is the macro and quant correlation above. I approve all four. The overlap table uses first-seven-year loadings, so it shows macro and quant with no shared bet even though they now move together. The audit log chain is intact.

What would change the view

  • Brennoch's rates loading back under 0.1. Relative value could then return to its equal-risk weight.
  • Brennoch's rates loading still near 0.78 at the next review. I would redeem relative value at its quarterly window.
  • Tarsen's momentum loading back near its earlier 0.10. I would then add to macro, the best sleeve, ahead of credit.
  • The rerun showing a worst stress episode deeper than 17.0%. I would revisit the size of the credit add.
  • A test of this book as the portable alpha sleeve showing a positive information ratio. I would then reopen the overlay.

Next steps

  1. Send Veyl Systematic a redemption notice for the next monthly window, on 30 days notice, taking the sleeve to 5%.
  2. Rerun the builder on the new weights.
  3. Subscribe the ten points to Ostvale Credit once the rerun is reviewed.
  4. Write to Brennoch about the 0.78 rates loading against its 0.1 cap, and to Tarsen about the momentum overlay against line 5 of its mandate.
  5. Ask Quillmere to explain its 0.32 momentum loading against its mandate's no-tilt language.
  6. Turn on volatility targeting (12-month lookback, 2x cap) and drawdown stops (10% trigger, 50% allocation for six months).
  7. Approve the four flags so they go into the risk note.
