Investment memo 02 · Fee and terms review

Ostrel Capital Partners VI

Date
30 Jun 2026
Fund
Ostrel Capital Partners VI, L.P. (initial close 15 October 2019)
Strategy
Private markets fund, past its investment period
LP commitment
$45m, 3.75% of the $1,200m fund
Prepared by
Cristian Arenas

Recommendation

I recommend we raise all three breaks with the GP and ask for a corrected Q2 2026 report. The fee items come to $108,281 for our account, and the $1,236,187 carry accrual should be reversed. No new commitment to Ostrel until the corrections are in.

Conditions for lifting the hold

  • The fee and offset differences are credited against our next capital call.
  • Q2 2026 is reissued with accrued carry at $0 and restated net IRR and TVPI.

Summary

Ostrel Capital Partners VI is a $1,200m fund with an initial close on 15 October 2019. Our $45m commitment is almost fully called. I recomputed every fee, offset and carry line in the four quarterly reports from Q3 2025 to Q2 2026 against the LPA excerpt. Three of the 14 checks broke, all in the GP's favour.

  • The Q3 2025 fee was charged on total commitments instead of net invested capital: $168,750 against $107,578.
  • The Q1 2026 offset was half of what section 8.2 requires: $47,109 against $94,219.
  • Q2 2026 accrues $1,236,187 of carry with no preferred return. Under the whole-fund waterfall nothing is due, with $7.05m of pref still owed. Restated net IRR is 4.1% against 3.3% reported.
  • Three errors in four reports in one direction look like a review problem at the GP, and the fund is weak anyway at 3.6% net without the subscription line. Both argue for holding new money.

The fund and our position

The fee basis steps down when the investment period ends on the fifth anniversary of the initial close, 15 October 2024. Net invested capital is the cost of unrealized investments at the start of each quarter.

Our position

At Q2 2026 we have contributed $44,631,563 and received $25,875,000. NAV before accrued carry is $24,937,500. Five deals are realized and seven remain. Net invested capital fell from $765m to $600m over the four quarters reviewed. Recomputed net fees since inception are $4.79m, 10.7% of our commitment.

The documents do not describe the team or the strategy, so I have no view on edge from this review. It answers whether the GP charges and reports what the LPA says.

Gross returns of 6.9% and 1.29x are modest. The GP's net figures recompute exactly from its own inputs, so the problem sits in one input, the carry accrual. Restating fees and carry to the LPA lifts net IRR to 4.1% and TVPI to 1.14x.

Ostrel VI does not report IRR without its subscription line. I rebuilt it by moving each call back to its investment date, about 180 days earlier. Net IRR falls to 3.6%, so the line adds 0.5 pp and leaves TVPI unchanged. DPI is 0.58x, so most of the value is still in NAV. PME was not measured.

European against American carry

At reported NAV, LPs hold $50.81m of distributions and NAV on $44.52m contributed (the report's $44.63m less the fee overcharge). The pref owed by now is $13.34m. Liquidation pays $6.29m of it and stops in tier (b) with $7.05m still owed. Nothing reaches the GP.

Deal by deal, the GP would already hold $1.42m on the five realized deals. Over 2000 simulated paths, with the seven unrealized deals exiting over four years at marks grown 6% a year with 30% volatility, the GP earns any carry in only 4% of paths under the LPA. Median final carry is $0.00m European against $2.34m American, and American is higher in 100% of paths. The Q2 accrual ignores the term that protects us most.

Portfolio fit

The corrections are small against the position: $108,281 of fees against $24.94m of NAV. Reversing the accrual adds $1,236,187 to our NAV and moves net TVPI from 1.11x to 1.14x. With $44.63m called on $45m, almost no unfunded exposure remains.

Fee drag is falling. The quarterly fee stepped down from $225,000 to $123,047 at the end of the investment period and was $84,375 by Q2 2026. Liquidity rests on the seven unrealized deals. The demo covers this fund only, so I have not measured pacing or concentration effects across our wider portfolio.

Diligence findings

I read five documents and extracted 37 fields, each tied to its page and line. Against verified ground truth, extraction accuracy and citation coverage are both 100%. All 3 planted issues were caught with no false flags on the 11 clean checks.

Break 1: Q3 2025 management fee

LPA 8.1 (p1 l6, l9) against the Q3 2025 report (p1 l7). After the investment period the fee is 1.5% of net invested capital, $765m at the start of the quarter, which ties to the ledger. Our share is $107,578. The report charges $168,750, the post-period rate on total commitments. Overcharge $61,172.

Break 2: Q1 2026 fee offset

LPA 8.2 (p1 l12) against the Q1 2026 report (p1 l8, l9). The fund received $2.8m of portfolio fees. Our offset is $105,000, capped at the gross fee of $94,219. The report offsets $47,109. Under-offset $47,110.

Break 3: Q2 2026 accrued carry

LPA 9.1(b) (p2 l4) and 9.4 (p2 l10) against the Q2 2026 report (p1 l21, l22). The report accrues 20% of $6.18m of net gains with no preferred return. Under the 9.1 waterfall nothing is due. The accrual understates our NAV and the reported net TVPI.

What tied

Net invested capital matches the ledger in all four quarters, and the other fees and offsets recompute exactly. The GP's 1.11x TVPI and 0.58x DPI tie to its own capital account, so they carry the wrong accrual through with no separate error.

Query letter and scope

The draft letter takes approved breaks only, with clause and report line for each. It asks the GP to credit $61,172 and $47,110 against the next capital call and to reverse the accrual and restate performance. It has not been sent.

Side letters and MFN terms are out of scope, and partnership expenses of $13,125 a quarter were not tested against a cap. I saw no reports before Q3 2025. The recomputed schedule charges the quarter ended 31 December 2024 at the full 2% rate, although the investment period ended on 15 October 2024. The excerpt is silent on that quarter, so I would ask.

What would change the view

  • The GP credits $108,281 and reissues Q2 2026 with carry at $0. A new commitment could then be judged on the fund's merits.
  • The GP shows a reading of section 9.4 that supports the $1,236,187 accrual. I do not expect one. It would need legal review.
  • Quarters since October 2024 show further breaks. A fourth error in the GP's favour would widen this into a review of the relationship.
  • Exits clear the $7.05m of pref still owed at NAV. Only then does a carry accrual become legitimate.
  • Evidence that carry was paid on realized deals, which would make the $1.42m a clawback claim.

Next steps

  1. Approve the three breaks and send the query letter, after correcting its fee total to $108,281.
  2. Ask for a corrected Q2 2026 report with carry under the 9.1 waterfall and restated net IRR, TVPI and DPI.
  3. Ask for the fee basis on every quarter since October 2024, including how Q4 2024 was charged.
  4. Ask the GP to confirm no carry has been distributed and to report IRR without the line.
  5. Request the full LPA and side letters.
  6. Audit the Fund VII reports next quarter.
