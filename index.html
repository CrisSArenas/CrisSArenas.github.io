About

I am a Quantitative Investment Professional and AI Engineer with 6+ years of experience across institutional investment management, quantitative research, and applied AI in the United States, Europe, and Latin America.

My work sits at the intersection of investment management and AI engineering, combining investment research and portfolio decision-making with the design and development of agentic AI systems, quantitative models, and investment analytics platforms.

My investment background spans private markets and multi-asset portfolio management, including private equity, private credit, hedge funds, public equities, fixed income, and real assets. My experience covers asset allocation, portfolio construction, manager selection and due diligence, investment research, and risk management. Within private markets, my work includes fund evaluation, commitment pacing, liquidity planning, and direct and co-investment analysis.

On the technical side, I design and develop agentic AI systems, multi-agent workflows, quantitative tools, and data infrastructure that support investment research, portfolio monitoring, manager analysis, and investment decisions. My focus is on building reliable systems that integrate into investment workflows, with appropriate controls, transparency, and human oversight.

I also conduct independent quantitative research, including published work on regime-aware deep learning for multi-asset allocation, available on SSRN.

I bring together institutional investment experience and hands-on AI engineering to develop practical solutions that improve how investment teams research, analyze, and manage portfolios.

01

Agentic AI systems

Several agents split the work the way an investment team does. One reads, one analyzes, one checks and one drafts, with hard checks between steps and a person approving anything that matters.

Orchestrator-worker designs with narrow agents, typed tool contracts and an independent verifier. Fixed workflows where the path is known, and planning only where the task varies, with step budgets and full traces.

  • Monitoring, research and reconciliation
  • Hard checks between every step
  • Human sign-off before anything moves
  • Scoped tools through Model Context Protocol
  • Schema-validated tool calls and budgets
  • Replayable traces and approval gates

02

Document intelligence

LPAs, GP reports, capital account statements, DDQs and CIMs read in minutes. Every figure is linked to the page and line it came from and checked before anyone uses it.

Layout-aware parsing, schema-first extraction into typed fields, span-level citations and cross-document reconciliation, with low-confidence fields routed to review.

  • Fees, terms, cash flows and NAVs
  • Works across GPs that report differently
  • Exceptions go to a person, and nothing is guessed
  • Scanned PDFs, multi-page tables and footnotes
  • Accuracy tracked per GP template
  • Roll-forward and cross-document checks

03

Data foundations

Portfolio, accounting, market data and document data brought into one tested, reconciled place, so every model and agent works from the same numbers.

Raw, clean and reconciled layers on Snowflake, Databricks or PostgreSQL, orchestrated with Airflow and modeled in dbt, with freshness and reconciliation tests, lineage and governed views for agents.

  • One master list of funds and GPs
  • Freshness and reconciliation checks
  • Access limited to what each user can see
  • Entity master and history kept as reported and as corrected
  • Tests on keys, freshness and reconciliations
  • Row-level permissions on governed views

Measured on the demos on this site

How these are measured
Extraction accuracy
Share of verified fields where the extracted value matches the confirmed answer within a stated tolerance.
Citation coverage
Share of extracted values whose page and line reference points to text that actually contains the value.
Planted issues caught
Errors placed in the documents on purpose, such as a wrong fee or a broken roll-forward, that the system flagged for review.
False-flag rate
Share of clean checks that were flagged anyway. A useful system keeps this near zero so reviewers trust the flags.
