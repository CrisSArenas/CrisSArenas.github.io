About

I have over six years of experience across institutional investment management, quantitative research, and AI engineering, combining hands-on portfolio management experience with the design and development of advanced AI systems.

My investment background spans public and private markets, including equities, fixed income, hedge funds, private equity, private credit, and real assets. My experience includes multi-asset portfolio management, strategic and tactical asset allocation, portfolio construction, investment strategy, manager selection, due diligence, and risk management. I have worked on managing portfolios, evaluating investment opportunities, developing investment strategies, and applying quantitative methods to investment decision-making.

My technical work focuses on architecting, developing, and deploying agentic AI systems, multi-agent workflows, and intelligent applications. I have experience designing AI architectures, orchestrating specialized agents, integrating large language models with enterprise data and tools, and developing retrieval, evaluation, and automation frameworks. My work covers the full lifecycle of AI systems, from architecture and development to testing, deployment, and production operations, with an emphasis on reliability, transparency, and human oversight.

I also conduct quantitative research, applying statistical modeling, machine learning, deep learning, and optimization techniques to financial markets and portfolio management. My research includes published work on regime-aware deep learning for multi-asset allocation, alongside the development of quantitative models for portfolio optimization, risk analysis, and investment strategy evaluation.

My background brings together institutional investment management, quantitative research, and AI systems engineering, with practical experience in both investment decision-making and building sophisticated AI solutions.

01

Agentic AI systems

Several agents split the work the way an investment team does. One reads, one analyzes, one checks and one drafts, with hard checks between steps and a person approving anything that matters.

Orchestrator-worker designs with narrow agents, typed tool contracts and an independent verifier. Fixed workflows where the path is known, and planning only where the task varies, with step budgets and full traces.

  • Monitoring, research and reconciliation
  • Hard checks between every step
  • Human sign-off before anything moves
  • Scoped tools through Model Context Protocol
  • Schema-validated tool calls and budgets
  • Replayable traces and approval gates

02

Document intelligence

LPAs, GP reports, capital account statements, DDQs and CIMs read in minutes. Every figure is linked to the page and line it came from and checked before anyone uses it.

Layout-aware parsing, schema-first extraction into typed fields, span-level citations and cross-document reconciliation, with low-confidence fields routed to review.

  • Fees, terms, cash flows and NAVs
  • Works across GPs that report differently
  • Exceptions go to a person, and nothing is guessed
  • Scanned PDFs, multi-page tables and footnotes
  • Accuracy tracked per GP template
  • Roll-forward and cross-document checks

Data foundations

Portfolio, accounting, market data and document data brought into one tested, reconciled place, so every model and agent works from the same numbers.

Raw, clean and reconciled layers on Snowflake, Databricks or PostgreSQL, orchestrated with Airflow and modeled in dbt, with freshness and reconciliation tests, lineage and governed views for agents.

  • One master list of funds and GPs
  • Freshness and reconciliation checks
  • Access limited to what each user can see
  • Entity master and history kept as reported and as corrected
  • Tests on keys, freshness and reconciliations
  • Row-level permissions on governed views

Measured on the demos on this site

How these are measured
Extraction accuracy
Share of verified fields where the extracted value matches the confirmed answer within a stated tolerance.
Citation coverage
Share of extracted values whose page and line reference points to text that actually contains the value.
Planted issues caught
Errors placed in the documents on purpose, such as a wrong fee or a broken roll-forward, that the system flagged for review.
False-flag rate
Share of clean checks that were flagged anyway. A useful system keeps this near zero so reviewers trust the flags.

Regime-Aware Transformer Architectures with Cross-Attention Regime Discovery for Multi-Asset Portfolio Construction

I test whether a transformer that learns market regimes and allocations together beats simpler allocators. On synthetic regime-switching markets every neural model beats the traditional baselines, though a plain LSTM does as well and none of the differences are significant.
