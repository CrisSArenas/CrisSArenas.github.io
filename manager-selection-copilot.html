How it works

Method, formulas, assumptions and limits
  • Concentration is the share of total gains from the best one and three deals. The loss ratio is the capital in deals below 1.0x.
  • The value bridge splits each deal's equity gain into EBITDA growth, the change in multiple and debt paydown.
  • IRR without the subscription line moves each call back to its investment date. Net returns are rebuilt from the deals and the stated fees, with carry from a whole-fund (European) waterfall. Net DPI is LP distributions after carry over paid-in capital, fees included.
  • Style analysis is a constrained regression of the fund's returns on style indices. Smoothed returns are unwound with an MA(2) model before computing the Sharpe ratio.
  • Smoothing pushes part of a fund's equity exposure into later months, so betas and the alpha regression include US large cap returns lagged one and two months, and the beta is the sum of the three coefficients.
  • Skill is tested with a block bootstrap of the alpha t-stat under zero alpha.

Formulas

top-N share = sum of N largest gains / total gains loss ratio = invested in deals with MOIC < 1 / invested EBITDA growth = (E1 - E0) m0 multiple = (m1 - m0) E1 deleveraging = D0 - D1 style: min ||r - Xw||^2, w >= 0, sum w = 1 R_obs,t = th0 R_t + th1 R_t-1 + th2 R_t-2 beta = b0 + b1 + b2 (US large cap at t, t-1, t-2)

Data and limits

  • Managers, companies and peers are made up.
  • A bootstrap p-value on one manager is weak evidence. Screening many managers at once turns up false positives.
