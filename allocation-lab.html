How it works

Method, formulas, assumptions and limits
  • Private assets enter the optimizer with Geltner-desmoothed volatility and correlations.
  • The optimizer maximizes the Sharpe ratio inside the policy ranges. It only accepts portfolios that still cover projected capital calls with liquid assets after a stress.
  • Black-Litterman blends equilibrium returns with views. The resampled frontier averages many optimizations on simulated returns.
  • Total portfolio mode measures active risk against a 70/30 reference portfolio and judges each candidate by what it adds to total risk and return.
  • Stress tests replay GFC, 2022 rate shock and COVID-style moves. Attribution uses the Brinson model.
